Why the yellow became a lime step

  • The doc named both colours "Accent 1", and they're only 11° apart in hue. Used side by side, they look like a mismatch rather than two choices.
  • As one family, the yellow gives the lime a darker partner for borders, hover and illustration shading.
  • Neither colour works as text on white (1.1:1 and 1.6:1), so both stay as fills.
